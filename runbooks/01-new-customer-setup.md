# Runbook 01: Set up a new customer

**When:** a contract has been signed in HubSpot.
**Goal:** the customer publishes their first schedule (T2L target: under a day; typical today: 1–3 days).
**Process:** `processes/customer-onboarding.md`

## Steps

1. Confirm the contract is signed in HubSpot.
2. Book a 45-minute setup call with the customer.
3. Get the venue and staff list from the customer as a CSV and import it with the importer.
   - If the import fails (non-Latin characters in names, or a file exported from Apple Numbers), follow `runbooks/02-importer-failures.md`.
4. Configure the pay rules for the customer's country.
5. Make sure the customer publishes their first schedule. This is where T2L stops.
6. Hold the 7-day check-in call.
7. Hand the customer over to support (`processes/support-escalation.md`).
