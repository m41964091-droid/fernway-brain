# What Fernway is

**Company:** remote-first B2B SaaS, founded 2024, registered in Berlin, with a team across five countries.

**Stage:** seed, raised early 2025. 15 people plus contractors.

## Product

*Fernway Shifts*: shift planning and labour-cost forecasting for independent café and restaurant groups with 3–20 locations. Managers build schedules; the tool checks them against local break and overtime rules and projects the wage bill before the week is published.

## Customers

About 40 accounts and about 140 paying venues, in Germany, the Netherlands and Ireland. One account can have many venues (see `glossary.md`).

## Pricing

- €39 per venue per month.
- 15% discount on annual prepay.
- Anything below that is a CEO decision: a **Mara-check**. Every pricing exception goes through Mara Voss, which is a known bottleneck.
- Some accounts are still on legacy pricing; when they move is undecided (see `open-questions.md`, item 5).

## Why customers pick Fernway

Competitors are built for enterprise chains and take weeks to configure. Fernway's pitch is that a venue is live in under a day. Onboarding speed is the whole positioning, which is why **T2L** (time to live) is the number the team watches.
